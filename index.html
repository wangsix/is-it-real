Prior先驗

If a team were exactly league average, its raw value after n games would fall inside the dark band 95% of the time and inside the light band 99.8% of the time, from game-to-game noise alone. The bands narrow as 1/√n. A team outside them is very unlikely to be average. A team inside them is not shown to be average: the games so far cannot yet separate it from average. The funnel uses no prior; the By stat view combines these numbers with a prior.如果一隊剛好是聯盟平均，單靠逐場雜訊，它打了 n 場後的原始數值有 95% 的機率落在深色帶內、99.8% 的機率落在淺色帶內。帶寬隨 1/√n 收窄。落在帶外的球隊幾乎不可能只是平均水準；落在帶內並不代表就是平均，只是目前的場數還無法把它和平均區分開。漏斗圖不使用先驗；「依數據」檢視才把這些數字和先驗結合。

Chip: clearly = the 95% range excludes this season's league average; likely = the 68% range does; otherwise the data cannot yet separate this team from average. Pace, 3PAr, FTR, AST% and Opp. 3PAr are style stats, so their chips say high or low, not better or worse. Click a stat to open it in the By stat view.標籤：明顯 = 95% 區間不含本季聯盟平均；可能 = 68% 區間不含；否則目前資料還無法把這隊和平均區分開。Pace、3PAr、FTR、AST%、Opp. 3PAr 是風格數據，標籤顯示偏高或偏低，而非好壞。點數據名稱可在「依數據」檢視中開啟。

± is one standard deviation of the team's true level under that prior: the true value is within estimate ± SD about 68% of the time. Δ is the change since last season relative to the league (the league-wide shift is removed), and z is Δ in units of sampling noise. Click a header to sort; click again to reverse. Click a team to open its card.± 是該先驗下球隊真實水準的一個標準差：約 68% 的機率真實值落在估計 ± SD 之內。Δ 是相對於聯盟的季間變化（已扣除全聯盟的整體變動），z 是 Δ 除以抽樣雜訊。點欄位標題排序，再點一次反向。點球隊開啟球隊卡。
