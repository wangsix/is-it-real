Prior

If a team were exactly league average, its raw value after n games would fall inside the dark band 95% of the time and inside the light band 99.8% of the time, from game-to-game noise alone. The bands narrow as 1/√n. A team outside them is very unlikely to be average. A team inside them is not shown to be average: the games so far cannot yet separate it from average. The funnel uses no prior; the By stat view combines these numbers with a prior.

Chip: clearly = the 95% range excludes this season's league average; likely = the 68% range does; otherwise the data cannot yet separate this team from average. Pace, 3PAr, FTR, AST% and Opp. 3PAr are style stats, so their chips say high or low, not better or worse. Click a stat to open it in the By stat view.

± is one standard deviation of the team's true level under that prior: the true value is within estimate ± SD about 68% of the time. Δ is the change since last season relative to the league (the league-wide shift is removed), and z is Δ in units of sampling noise. Click a header to sort; click again to reverse.
