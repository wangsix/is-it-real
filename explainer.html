Is it real? · NBA team stats, 2015-16 to 2024-25是真的嗎？ · NBA 球隊數據，2015-16 至 2024-25

Signal, Noise and k訊號、雜訊與 k

How many games before a team's numbers mean something? Each stat has a one-number answer, k. This page shows where it comes from and how to turn a hot start into a fair estimate.

一支球隊要打幾場，數據才有意義？每項數據都有一個數字答案：k。本頁說明它從哪裡來，以及如何把開季的火燙（或冰冷）表現換算成合理的實力估計。

The short version一分鐘版本

  1. Every stat after n games is the team's true level plus luck. In a single game the luck is about three times as large as the real gap between teams.
  2. Averaging over games cancels luck. k is the number of games after which a team's average is half real, half luck. For Net Rtg k ≈ 9 games; for opponents' 3-point percentage, over 150.
  3. So early in the season, blend the team's numbers with a sensible starting guess, such as the league average or last season pulled toward average. The rankings page does this blend and shows how sure it is.
  1. 打了 n 場後的任何數據，都是球隊的真實水準加上運氣。單看一場，運氣的影響大約是球隊之間真實差距的三倍。
  2. 平均越多場，運氣就互相抵銷越多。k 是讓球隊的平均值變成「一半真實、一半運氣」所需的場數。Net Rtg 的 k 約 9 場；對手三分命中率超過 150 場。
  3. 所以季初應該把球隊的數據和一個合理的起點加權混合，例如聯盟平均，或往平均拉回的上季表現。排名頁做的就是這件事，並標出估計有多確定。

Motivation動機

One game is three times as noisy as the gap between teams單場雜訊是球隊差距的三倍

In short. One game's point margin swings about 14 points (SD) around what the team is really worth, while teams' true strengths differ by only about 5 points (SD). A single game tells you very little.

簡單說：單場比賽的分差，會在球隊真實實力上下擺盪約 14 分（標準差）；而球隊之間的真實實力只差約 5 分（標準差）。單看一場，幾乎看不出什麼。

Every stat you see after n games is true level + noise. For point margin, the noise in a single game has a standard deviation of 13.9 points; true team strength across the league has an SD of only 4.7 points. One result says little about how good the team is, and only averaging over many games separates the two.

n 場後看到的每一項數據，都是真實水準 + 雜訊。以得失分差為例，單場雜訊的標準差是 13.9 分，而全聯盟球隊真實實力的標準差只有 4.7 分。單一結果幾乎無法說明球隊有多強，只有平均夠多場，才能把兩者分開。

Noise, defined. Take one team-season, subtract that team's season-average margin from each of its 82 game margins, and take the SD of those 82 residuals; pool across all team-seasons. That is σe = 13.9. It is not the SD of all game margins in the league (≈ 14.7), which also contains the differences between teams. Signal, σT = 4.7, is the SD of season-average margins across teams after removing the part of their spread that is itself sampling noise: the observed SD of the 30 season means is 4.94 (averaged over the 8 seasons), but an 82-game mean carries noise variance σe²/82 = 13.9²/82 = 2.37, so σT² = 4.94² − 2.37 = 22.1 and σT = 4.7.

雜訊的定義。取一個球隊球季，把 82 場的分差各自減去該隊本季的平均分差，再算這 82 個殘差的標準差；把所有球隊球季合併計算，就得到 σe = 13.9。它不是全聯盟所有比賽分差的標準差（≈ 14.7），後者還包含了球隊之間的差異。訊號 σT = 4.7 是各隊季平均分差的標準差，再扣掉其中本身屬於抽樣雜訊的部分：30 隊季平均的實際標準差是 4.94（8 季平均），但 82 場的平均本身帶有 σe²/82 = 13.9²/82 = 2.37 的雜訊變異，所以 σT² = 4.94² − 2.37 = 22.1，σT = 4.7。

Sections 1 and 2 use point margin per game, 2015-16 to 2024-25. From section 4 on, the page uses Net Rtg, the same margin per 100 possessions; its k is almost the same (8.5–8.6).

第 1、2 節用的是每場得失分差（2015-16 至 2024-25）。第 4 節起改用 Net Rtg，也就是每 100 回合的得失分差；兩者的 k 幾乎相同（8.5–8.6）。

Calculation計算

k: the exchange rate between games and knowledgek：比賽場數與資訊之間的匯率

In short. k is the number of games after which a team's average is half real and half luck. After 2k games it is two-thirds real; after 9k games, 90%. Small k: the stat settles fast. Large k: it stays noisy for a long time.

簡單說：k 是讓球隊的平均值變成一半真實、一半運氣所需的場數。打到 2k 場時三分之二是真實，9k 場時 90%。k 小，數據很快就穩定；k 大，數據要很久才擺脫雜訊。

Take a team's average margin over its first n games. Two things are mixed into that number: the team's true level, which varies between teams with SD σT = 4.7, and leftover noise, which averaging shrinks from 13.9 to 13.9/√n (5.3 after 7 games, 2.5 after 30). Line the 30 teams up by their n-game averages: part of their spread is real, part is leftover noise. Reliability is the real share:

取一支球隊前 n 場的平均分差。這個數字混了兩樣東西：球隊的真實水準（球隊之間的標準差 σT = 4.7），以及殘留雜訊，平均會把它從 13.9 縮小到 13.9/√n（7 場後 5.3，30 場後 2.5）。把 30 隊依 n 場平均排開：其中一部分差距是真的，一部分是殘留雜訊。信度就是真實的那一份：

rel(n) = σT² / (σT² + σe²/n)  =  n / (n + k),   k = σe² / σT²
n = 7: true variance 22.1, leftover noise variance 194/7 = 27.7 → rel = 22.1 / (22.1 + 27.7) = 0.44n = 7：真實變異 22.1，殘留雜訊變異 194/7 = 27.7 → rel = 22.1 / (22.1 + 27.7) = 0.44
the same via k: 7 / (7 + 8.8) = 0.44. Dividing top and bottom by σT² is all that happened.用 k 算也一樣：7 / (7 + 8.8) = 0.44。只是把分子分母同除以 σT²。

k is measured in games. It is how many games of a team's own results carry as much information as knowing only that it is an NBA team, that is, the league's spread of strength. At n = k the average is 50% signal; at n = 2.3k, 70%; at n = 9k, 90%.

k 的單位是場。它表示：一支球隊自己要打多少場，提供的資訊才等於「只知道它是一支 NBA 球隊」（也就是聯盟的實力分布）。n = k 時平均值有 50% 是訊號；n = 2.3k 時 70%；n = 9k 時 90%。

Method 1 · moments方法一・動差

8.8

Pooled within-season variance of single-game margins (noise) divided by the between-team variance of season means minus its own sampling share (signal).

季內單場分差的合併變異（雜訊），除以各隊季平均之間的變異再扣掉其抽樣部分（訊號）。

Method 2 · split-half方法二・折半

8.5

Shuffle each season's 82 games into two halves of 41, correlate the two half-season means across teams (r = 0.83), and invert r = 41/(41+k).

把每季 82 場隨機分成兩半各 41 場，計算兩個半季平均在各隊之間的相關（r = 0.83），再由 r = 41/(41+k) 反推 k。

Method 3 · curve fit方法三・曲線擬合

11.7

Fit k to the real curve of first N games vs the rest of the season. It comes out higher because teams change during a season (trades, rest, tanking), so a model with one fixed level per team is slightly too optimistic.

用實際的「前 N 場 vs 剩餘賽季」曲線擬合 k。結果偏高，因為球隊在季中會變（交易、輪休、擺爛），所以「每隊一個固定水準」的模型略為樂觀。

Interpretation解讀

Nin and Nout: two ways to ask "how many games?"Nin 與 Nout：問「要幾場」的兩種方式

In short. The well-known 2017 table compares a team's first N games with its full-season stats, which include those same N games, so early numbers look more trustworthy than they are. Comparing with the games still to come is the fair test of prediction, and it needs more games: 16 instead of 10 for Net Rtg.

簡單說：2017 年那張知名表格，拿球隊前 N 場和全季數據比較，但全季數據本身就包含這 N 場，所以早期數字看起來比實際可靠。拿來和「之後還沒打的比賽」比較，才是預測能力的公平檢驗，而且需要更多場：Net Rtg 從 10 場變成 16 場。

In “When can we trust a team's stats?” (Nylon Calculus, December 2017), Krishna Narsu asked, using the four seasons before: after how many games does the correlation between a team's stats so far and its full-season stats pass r² = 0.5, the point where, as he put it, “the skill outweighs the noise”? That is Nin. But the full-season number contains the first N games, so the correlation is inflated and reaches 1 at N = 82 for any stat. Nout correlates the first N games with the remaining games instead.

Krishna Narsu 在 Nylon Calculus 的〈When can we trust a team's stats?〉（2017 年 12 月）中，用之前四季的資料問：球隊至今數據與全季數據的相關，要幾場後 r² 才超過 0.5？也就是他所說「實力壓過雜訊」的那一點。這就是 Nin。但全季數據包含前 N 場，相關因此被灌水，任何數據到 N = 82 都會達到 1。Nout 改成拿前 N 場和剩下的比賽比較。

Why Nout is often a dash為什麼 Nout 常常是「—」

The best any split can do is half-season vs half-season: r²max = (41/(41+k))². That exceeds 0.5 only when k < 17. For 3PT% (k = 48) or Def Rtg (k = 19) no N ever qualifies. The dash means the spread between teams is small compared with game noise, not that the stat is useless.

任何切法最好也只能是半季對半季：r²max = (41/(41+k))²。只有 k < 17 時才會超過 0.5。3PT%（k = 48）或 Def Rtg（k = 19）不論 N 是多少都達不到。「—」代表球隊間的差距相對於單場雜訊太小，不代表這項數據沒用。

Read k instead of a threshold看 k，不必看門檻

Thresholds are conventions: Narsu used r² = 0.5, and baseball “stabilization” studies often use a split-half reliability of 0.7. k turns any reliability target t into games: n = k·t/(1−t). Under a fixed-level model Nin = 41k/(41+k), so Nin and k carry the same information, and only k also tells you how much to shrink.

門檻只是慣例：Narsu 用 r² = 0.5，棒球的「穩定化」研究常用折半信度 0.7。k 可以把任何信度目標 t 換算成場數：n = k·t/(1−t)。在固定水準模型下 Nin = 41k/(41+k)，所以 Nin 和 k 帶有相同資訊，而只有 k 還能告訴你該往平均拉回多少。

All the stats全部數據

Style settles in a week; opponents' 3-point shooting never does風格一週就定型；對手三分命中率整季都定不下來

In short. Choices a team makes, such as how fast it plays and how many threes it takes, show up within a week. Results of shots take much longer, and opponents' 3-point percentage cannot be told apart from luck within a season.

簡單說：球隊自己的選擇，例如節奏快慢、出手多少三分，一週內就看得出來。投籃的結果要久得多，而對手三分命中率在一季之內都無法和運氣分開。

Pace、3PAr、FTR、AST%、Opp. 3PAr 是風格數據：數值高不代表較好或較差。其餘衡量的是好壞。Narsu 表中的 KOBE、SHAC 投籃品質數據需要球員追蹤資料，這裡沒有收錄。

Stat definitions數據定義

Team totals for the season so far, then the ratio (not an average of game values). Possessions use Basketball-Reference's estimate, averaged over both teams. Names follow Narsu's table; Basketball-Reference writes eFG%, FTr, ORtg / DRtg and TRB%. See the Basketball-Reference glossary.

先把本季至今的球隊總數加總，再取比值（不是把每場數值平均）。回合數使用 Basketball-Reference 的估計公式，取兩隊平均。名稱沿用 Narsu 的表格；Basketball-Reference 寫作 eFG%、FTr、ORtg / DRtg、TRB%。詳見 Basketball-Reference 名詞解釋。

Using it實際應用

A prior is a measurement worth K games先驗就是一筆值 K 場的測量

In short. Before the season you already know something: last season's results, who is still on the roster. Treat that knowledge as if it were K extra games and average it with this season's games. The better a starting guess has predicted in the past, the more games it is worth.

簡單說：開季前你已經知道一些事：上季的成績、誰還在陣中。把這些資訊當成額外的 K 場比賽，和本季的比賽一起平均。一個起點過去預測得越準，它就值越多場。

Shrinkage pools two measurements of the same thing by their precision: this season's average over n games, and a prior guess μ whose error has SD τ.

收縮估計依精確度合併同一件事的兩筆測量：本季 n 場的平均，以及一個誤差標準差為 τ 的先驗猜測 μ。

estimate = (n · x̄ + K · μ) / (n + K),   K = σe² / τ²

An n-game average has sampling variance σe²/n. A prior with error variance τ² is therefore exactly as informative as K = σe²/τ² games. “Worth 14 games” means the prior's error SD equals the noise of a 14-game average. The plain k is the special case where the prior is the league itself (μ = league average, τ = σT).

n 場平均的抽樣變異是 σe²/n。所以誤差變異為 τ² 的先驗，提供的資訊剛好等於 K = σe²/τ² 場。「值 14 場」的意思是：這個先驗的誤差標準差，等於 14 場平均的雜訊。原本的 k 是特例：先驗就是聯盟本身（μ = 聯盟平均，τ = σT）。

League avg聯盟平均

μ = 0, τ = σT. Assumes nothing about the team. Pulls everyone toward average but keeps this season's order. This is classic “padding”: add k games of league average to every team.

μ = 0，τ = σT。對球隊不做任何假設。把每隊往平均拉，但保留本季的排序。這就是經典的「墊場」做法：替每隊加上 k 場聯盟平均。

Last season上季

μ = last season's value, τ = σT by choice. A half-measure: it uses last year's number but claims no more certainty than knowing nothing, so it gets the same K as the league prior. It ignores off-season changes.

μ = 上季數值，τ 刻意設為 σT。折衷做法：用了去年的數字，卻不比一無所知更有把握，所以 K 和聯盟平均先驗相同。它忽略休賽季的變化。

Regressed回歸上季

μ = 0.58 × last season, τ = 3.8. What last season actually predicts about this one, fitted on ten pairs of seasons. Worth 14 games, so after 7 games it still holds 67% of the weight.

μ = 0.58 × 上季，τ = 3.8。上季對本季實際的預測力，以十組相鄰球季擬合。值 14 場，所以打了 7 場後它仍占 67% 的權重。

Continuity陣容延續

Like Regressed, but ρ and τ depend on c, the share of last season's minutes still on the roster: ρ(c) = 0.27 + 0.47c. An intact roster (c ≈ 1) carries over more and is worth about 26 games; a rebuilt one (c ≈ 0.3) about 10.

同回歸上季，但 ρ 與 τ 依 c 而定：c 是上季上場時間仍留在陣中的比例，ρ(c) = 0.27 + 0.47c。陣容完整（c ≈ 1）延續較多，約值 26 場；大幅重組（c ≈ 0.3）約值 10 場。

Your projection自訂預測

Any pre-season number, such as a projection model or a betting line, with its own K = σe²/τ². A projection with error SD 3.5 is worth about 16 games. Not on the site; the command-line tool takes one with --prior-csv.

任何季前數字，例如預測模型或盤口，都有自己的 K = σe²/τ²。誤差標準差 3.5 的預測約值 16 場。網站上沒有這個選項；命令列工具可用 --prior-csv 提供。

The cards use Net Rtg, whose league average is 0. In general every prior is centred on this season's league average plus the team's gap from last season's league average: μ = μnow + ρ·(last − μlast). A league-wide shift, such as turnovers up 1.1 points in 2026-27, then moves every prior with it instead of making all 30 teams look changed.

上面的卡片用 Net Rtg，聯盟平均為 0。一般來說，每個先驗都以本季聯盟平均，加上球隊上季相對聯盟平均的差距為中心：μ = μ本季 + ρ·(上季 − μ上季)。全聯盟的整體變動（例如 2026-27 失誤率上升 1.1 個百分點）會讓所有先驗一起移動，而不會讓 30 隊看起來都變了。

All of them estimate the team's current true level, not a season total. A projected final = (n·raw + (82−n)·estimate)/82.

它們估計的都是球隊目前的真實水準，不是全季總計。預測的全季數值 = (n·本季 + (82−n)·估計值)/82。

Three models三種模型

Season average, current form, full strength季平均、近況、完整戰力

In short. Season average counts every game the same. Current form lets a team's level drift, so recent games count a little more and the ranges are wider; back-tests show those wider ranges are the correctly sized ones. Full strength asks how good the team is when its usual players are available.

簡單說：季平均讓每場比賽的權重相同。近況允許球隊水準漂移，所以近期比賽的權重略高、區間較寬；回測顯示，較寬的區間才是大小正確的區間。完整戰力問的是：主力都在時，這支球隊有多強。

The shrinkage above treats the season so far as repeated measurements of one fixed level. Teams are not fixed: rotations settle, players get hurt, trades happen. The rankings page offers three readings of the same games, all with the same priors.

上面的收縮估計，把本季至今視為對同一個固定水準的重複測量。但球隊不是固定的：輪替會磨合、球員會受傷、會有交易。排名頁對同一批比賽提供三種解讀，先驗都相同。

Season average季平均

One constant level; every game counts the same. Estimate = w·(this season) + (1−w)·prior, range σe/√(G+K).

一個固定水準；每場比賽權重相同。估計 = w·（本季）+ (1−w)·先驗，區間 σe/√(G+K)。

Current form近況

The level may drift a little each game: θt = θt−1 + η, Var η = q. A Kalman filter discounts old games and widens the range, because today's level is pinned down less well than the average.

水準每場可以小幅漂移：θt = θt−1 + η，Var η = q。卡爾曼濾波會降低舊比賽的權重並放寬區間，因為「今天的水準」比「季平均」更難確定。

Full strength完整戰力

Current form after crediting each game for the talent that sat out. Answers “how good is this roster with its normal rotation”, not “what will the record be”. Net and Off Rtg only.

近況模型，再把每場缺陣球員的戰力補回。回答的是「這套陣容以正常輪替出賽有多強」，而不是「最後戰績會如何」。僅限 Net Rtg 與 Off Rtg。

How much drift?漂移有多大？

q is fitted per stat by maximum likelihood of one-step-ahead predictions on 2016–2025, using values relative to each game night's league average so that league-wide trends are not mistaken for team drift. With q = 0 the filter reproduces the season average exactly.

q 依各項數據分別以 2016–2025 的「預測下一場」最大概似估計擬合；數值都先減去當晚的聯盟平均，避免把全聯盟的趨勢誤認為球隊漂移。q = 0 時，濾波結果和季平均完全相同。

Full strength: counting the missing talent完整戰力：缺陣戰力怎麼算

For each game, on-floor talent = Σ rp · minutes/48, where rp is the player's Box Plus/Minus (BPM: points per 100 possessions above an average player, from Basketball-Reference; OBPM for Off Rtg) over the last three seasons, weighted 1 / 0.4 / 0.2 by minutes, shrunk toward halfway between replacement level and average with 500 minutes of evidence, and moved by a typical change for the player's age. A player who missed last season is still rated from the seasons before. A player's normal minutes are the median over the games the player was available for, with a coach's-decision DNP counting as 0; a player who has been unavailable all along gets last season's minutes. Full strength fills 240 minutes from the most-used player down, so a returning starter pushes out the end of the bench. The game's value is moved by β × (full − on-floor talent); β is the within-team slope of results on missing talent, fitted on 2022-23 to 2024-25 (leave-one-season-out in the back-test).

每場的在場戰力 = Σ rp · 上場分鐘/48，rp 是球員近三季的 Box Plus/Minus（BPM：每 100 回合比平均球員多貢獻的分數，取自 Basketball-Reference；Off Rtg 用 OBPM），三季依上場分鐘按 1 / 0.4 / 0.2 加權，以 500 分鐘的證據量往替補水準與平均之間的中點收縮，再依年齡加上一般的逐年變化。上季整季缺陣的球員，仍以更早的球季評分。球員的正常上場時間，是他可出賽場次的分鐘中位數，教練決定不上場（DNP）記為 0；整季都無法出賽的球員則用上季的場均分鐘。完整戰力從上場最多的球員往下填滿 240 分鐘，所以先發回歸會擠掉板凳末端，而不是稀釋所有人。每場數值調整 β × (完整戰力 − 在場戰力)；β 是球隊內部「結果對缺陣戰力」的斜率，以 2022-23 至 2024-25 擬合（回測時每次留出一季）。

A prior describes a typical season, absences included. At full strength the priors, the league average and the dashed line all move up by β × the league's average missing talent so far, about +0.8 Net Rtg in 2026-27, so “better than average” still means better than an average team at full strength.

先驗描述的是一般球季，本來就包含缺陣。所以在完整戰力下，先驗、聯盟平均與虛線都會上移 β × 目前全聯盟的平均缺陣戰力（2026-27 約 +0.8 Net Rtg），讓「優於平均」仍然是指優於一支完整戰力的平均球隊。

A test of the method方法的檢驗

Quarters: more observations, not more information分節：觀察變多，資訊沒變多

In short. Splitting games into quarters gives four times the numbers but no extra information. Quarters are not equal samples: fourth quarters carry the least signal because of garbage time and resting starters.

簡單說：把比賽拆成四節，數字變成四倍，資訊卻沒有增加。各節並不是等價的樣本：第四節因為垃圾時間與主力休息，訊號最少。

Splitting a game into four quarters quadruples the rows but not the possessions, and the pieces are not equal. Signal per quarter falls through the game. The “fourth-quarter team” deviation is weakly real, and mostly a side effect of big leads and bench minutes.

把一場比賽拆成四節，資料列變成四倍，回合數卻沒變，而且四節並不相等。每節的訊號隨比賽進行遞減。「第四節球隊」的偏差有一點是真的，但大多是大幅領先、換上替補的副作用。
